Child Safety Standards & Anti-CSAE Policy

App Name:
Ammini
Developer:
Anjaneya Sivan

Ammini has a zero-tolerance stance toward Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Ammini is a local client application designed to play video files from your Telegram account. Ammini does not host, store, index, or distribute media content on external servers.

Reporting & Abuse Handling

  • Telegram Platform Content: Because all media accessible through Ammini originates directly from Telegram's cloud network, any illegal content hosted on Telegram should be reported directly to Telegram via their official abuse reporting channels (abuse@telegram.org or stopCA@telegram.org) or through Telegram's in-app moderation mechanisms.
  • In-App Feedback & Safety Contact: If you identify any safety concerns or child exploitation issues related to the Ammini application, please contact our child safety point of contact directly through the project's issue tracker at https://github.com/anjaneyasivan/ammini/issues.
